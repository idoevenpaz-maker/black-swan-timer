BLACK SWAN
Random Interval Timer
HOW IT WORKS
⏱️
Total duration
How long the whole workout lasts, in minutes. Half minutes work too (e.g. 7.5).
🎯
Intervals
Choose how many intervals you want. The app randomly decides when each one starts and how long it lasts. The schedule stays hidden, so you never know what's coming next.
📏
Min / max duration
The shortest and longest an interval can be, in seconds (minimum 10). There's always at least 10 seconds of rest between intervals, and at least 8 seconds before the first one.
🔊
Audio cues
5 beeps count down before each interval starts and ends, then a sound marks the start and the end. A double tone marks the end of the workout.
🟢
Status colours
Green = interval  ·  Blue = rest  ·  Violet = paused
👁️
Reveal
While hidden, the big clock counts up through each interval and rest. Tap Reveal during a workout to see the full schedule and count down instead.
💾
Save workout
Tap Save Workout to store your current settings under a name. Tap a saved workout to load it, or ✕ to delete it.
💡
Pro tip
Put your phone in your pocket and listen through earphones. The cues keep playing with the screen locked, and you can pause from the lock screen.
